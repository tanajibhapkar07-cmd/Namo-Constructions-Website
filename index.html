About the organisation

Two practical divisions. One standard of quality.

Namo Constructions is a Pune based organisation working in two clear areas — construction and technology. Every project, whether a residential building or a software product, is planned carefully, executed with clear communication and delivered with commitment.

Division 01 — Namo Constructions

Residential construction & engineering services in Pune

Services are provided in Pune and surrounding areas. Every project is planned from drawings, executed under supervision and checked for quality and safety through the work cycle.

House Construction

Turnkey residential construction — footing, structure, plaster, finishes and handover, carried out as per approved drawings and site conditions.

RCC Work

Reinforced cement concrete work including footings, columns, beams, slabs and staircases, executed to structural drawings and detailing.

Design & Build

Coordinated planning of drawings, materials and site work under one point of responsibility, so design and execution stay aligned.

Structural Supervision

Engineering supervision of alignment, levels, reinforcement fixing and concrete stages, with site records maintained at each step.

Quality & Safety

Material checks, curing practice, finishing standards and site safety measures followed consistently through the work cycle.

Project Commitment

A defined scope, a realistic schedule and regular progress updates, with communication maintained until the project is handed over.
