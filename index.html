About the organisation

One organisation.
Two practical divisions.

Namo Constructions runs two related but distinct lines of work. The construction division delivers residential building and engineering-led project execution. The technology division develops practical software and AI applications that solve everyday problems.

Both divisions run on the same principle: understand the real requirement first, then do the work properly. That means sound engineering on site, and software that works reliably on the devices people actually own.

  • Residential construction and RCC services across Pune and nearby areas
  • Software products developed and maintained by the same organisation
  • Direct communication with the people doing the work
  • Clear scope, clear documentation, clear handover

Our two divisions

Namo Constructions

Residential construction and engineering-led project execution: house construction, RCC structural work, design-and-build delivery, structural supervision and quality and safety control on site.

ClueTech Tecnnology

Practical software and AI applications developed for Android and Flutter, focused on everyday utilities such as photo resizing, background removal and puzzle-based entertainment.

What we stand for Values that guide both divisions

Quality

Work is checked as it progresses, not only at the end. Materials, dimensions and finishing are reviewed against the agreed scope.

Safety

Site safety, disciplined sequencing and safe working methods are treated as part of the work, not an add-on.

Engineering

Structural decisions are made with engineering reasoning behind them, so the finished building behaves properly for years.

Technology

Construction and software are treated as two sides of one capability set, and technology is used to improve both.

Innovation

New methods are adopted when they are practical and verifiable, not because they are new.

Communication

Progress, cost implications and changes are communicated early, so decisions are never left to guesswork.

Project commitment

Agreed scope, agreed timeline and a documented handover. We take responsibility for what we commit to, and we tell you early when something needs to change.

Namo Constructions

Residential construction and RCC engineering services

We work as a residential construction and engineering service business in Pune and surrounding areas. The scope covers planning inputs, structural execution, finishing coordination and handover — handled either as separate stages or as a single design-and-build responsibility.

House Construction

End-to-end residential home construction, from foundation and structure through roofing, plastering, flooring, painting and finishing, coordinated with the owner's requirements and budget at every stage.

  • Independent houses and duplex homes
  • Bungalows and residential expansions
  • Renovation and additions

Available across Pune and surrounding areas.

RCC Work

Reinforced cement concrete work carried out for foundations, columns, beams, slabs, staircases and retaining elements — with correct cover, proper reinforcement placement and disciplined curing.

  • Footings, plinth beams, columns and slabs
  • Staircases, chajjas and lintels
  • Water tank and retaining structures

Executed to the structural drawings agreed for the project.

Design & Build

A single point of responsibility covering planning and coordination of design with construction execution, so drawings, cost and site reality stay aligned instead of shifting responsibility between parties.

  • Layout, planning and design coordination
  • Structural and architectural drawings
  • Single-point budgeting and execution

Scope fixed in writing before work begins.

Structural Supervision

Technical oversight of structural execution: review of reinforcement and formwork before concreting, stage-wise checks, and prompt reporting of any site condition that needs an engineering decision.

  • Pre-pour reinforcement and shuttering checks
  • Stage-wise quality checkpoints
  • Documented technical observations

Available as part of a build or as a standalone review.

Quality & Safety

Site safety practices and material discipline applied consistently: correct material storage, safe access and scaffolding, sequenced work, and protection of completed work during later stages.

  • Material and workmanship checks
  • Safe access, scaffolding and sequencing
  • Protection of completed stages

Practices followed on every active site.

Project Commitment

Defined scope, realistic timelines and clear communication throughout the project. You know what is agreed, what is in progress and what happens next at every point of the build.

  • Written scope and transparent estimates
  • Regular progress updates
  • Documented handover and after-sales support

Estimates are project-specific and confirmed after site study.

How the work proceeds A straightforward sequence from first call to handover

  1. Enquiry & site visit

    We discuss your requirement and budget, then visit the site to understand the plot, access, soil and existing conditions.

  2. Plan & design inputs

    Layout and structural planning are prepared, material and method choices are outlined, and the scope is confirmed in writing.

  3. Execution & RCC works

    Foundation, RCC structure, masonry and finishing are executed stage by stage, with technical checks before each structural pour.

  4. Handover & support

    Completion checks, snag rectification, documentation and handover — followed by support for any post-completion requirements.

Product status is shown honestly. Applications labelled Available on Google Play are published and can be installed. Products still in development are labelled Coming Soon. We never publish a download link for an application that has not been released.

Android

Mobile-first Android applications

The Clue AI range is designed for Android smartphones first. The applications are structured around small screens, efficient storage use and behaviour that stays predictable on entry-level as well as current devices.

  • Designed for small screens

    Single-handed layouts, readable type sizes and touch targets sized for everyday use.

  • AI where it helps

    Artificial intelligence is applied to image tasks where it reduces effort — not added as decoration.

  • Lightweight by design

    Small download size and low memory use, so the apps remain usable on modest hardware and limited data plans.

  • Play Store availability

    Applications appear here only after publication. Until then they are listed as Coming Soon with no download link.

Flutter

Flutter: the next software platform

Flutter is the intended direction for ClueTech Tecnnology’s future mobile software. It allows a single codebase to target Android and other platforms while keeping interface behaviour and appearance consistent across devices.

No Flutter application has been released yet. Flutter work is at the development stage. Nothing on this page should be read as a public download or a released product.

  • Cross-platform codebaseOne Flutter codebase targeting Android first, with room to extend.
  • Shared UI layerConsistent screens and interaction patterns across supported devices.
  • Reusable product logicImage processing and game logic structured to be shared between products.
  • Release readinessTesting, store assets and listing preparation before any public release.

Why Flutter for ClueTech

  • A single codebase instead of separate Android and other-platform codebases
  • Consistent interface rendering across a wide range of Android devices
  • Good fit for image tools and puzzle games with custom interaction
  • Easier long-term maintenance as the product range grows

Status: in development. Publication is announced on this website once a release exists.
