Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy applies to this website and, where stated, to individual ClueTech Technology applications. Each published Android application is also provided with its own application-specific privacy policy on its Google Play listing, which takes precedence for data handled inside that application.

1. Who we are

Namo Constructions is a construction and engineering organisation based in Pune, Maharashtra, India, operating two divisions: Namo Constructions (residential construction and engineering services) and ClueTech Technology (software and AI applications for Android and Flutter).

For the purposes of this policy, "we", "us" and "our" refer to Namo Constructions / ClueTech Technology. Business contact details are listed in the Contact section of this website.

2. Information we collect

We collect only the information that is needed to respond to you and to operate the website. Depending on how you interact with us, this may include:

  • Information you provide — your name, email address, phone number, subject and message when you submit an enquiry, or details you share while discussing a construction project.
  • Technical information — basic technical data such as browser type, device type, approximate region and the pages visited, where analytics are enabled.
  • Information from published applications — data handled inside a ClueTech application, as described in that application's own privacy policy.

We do not ask for sensitive personal data through this website, and we do not buy or sell personal information.

3. Website enquiries

The contact form on this website is validated in your browser and opens your own email application with the message pre-filled. The website itself does not store, save or transmit form submissions to a database, and no message data is retained by this website.

When you choose to email or telephone us, the information you send is received through our email and telephone services and is used only to handle your enquiry, prepare a quotation if requested, and maintain related correspondence.

Business contact details are published as plain text on this website. The site uses no click-to-call links, no directions or mapping links, and no third-party dialler or map widget, so contacting us does not involve any intermediary processing your number or location.

4. Cookies

Cookies are small text files placed on your device by a website. This website is built to work without advertising or profiling cookies.

  • Essential cookies — may be set by our hosting provider for basic security, load balancing and session integrity. These are required for the website to function.
  • Preference cookies — may remember choices such as a dismissed menu or language preference.
  • Third-party cookies — may be set only if a third-party feature (such as an analytics tool) is enabled on the site.

You can block or delete cookies through your browser settings at any time. Blocking essential cookies may affect how the website behaves.

5. Analytics

We may use privacy-focused web analytics to understand aggregate traffic to the website — for example which pages are visited and whether the site loads correctly on mobile devices. Analytics data is used in aggregate form to improve the website's content, speed and usability.

Where an analytics provider is enabled, it processes information such as your IP address and browser details on our behalf under its own data processing terms. You can opt out of many analytics tools by using browser add-ons or enabling Do Not Track signals where supported.

6. Advertising

This website does not currently display third-party advertising and does not use advertising cookies or personalised ad networks.

If advertising is introduced in the future (for example Google AdSense or an equivalent network), this policy will be updated before that change takes effect, and any advertising partner will be disclosed here together with its opt-out controls and advertising-settings links.

7. Third-party services

We rely on a small number of third-party providers to run the website and our business communications:

  • Web hosting and delivery — hosting your site files and serving them over HTTPS.
  • Email — used when you send us an enquiry or a message.
  • Web fonts — typeface files may be served by a font provider; the provider receives your browser's standard request data.
  • Google Play — application listings, downloads and update delivery are handled by Google under Google's policies.

These providers process data under their own terms and privacy policies. We recommend reviewing them.

This website does not embed maps, video players, social media feeds or click-to-call services. Our address, email address and telephone number are shown as plain text and are not linked to any third-party map or dialler service, so browsing the site does not send your information to those providers.

8. AI features and AI APIs

Some ClueTech Technology products are described as AI-powered. Where an application uses an AI service or API to perform a task (for example analysing an image to resize or remove a background), the data required for that task may be transmitted to the AI provider for processing.

  • Only the information needed to complete the requested task is sent — for example the image you selected.
  • Private API keys, service credentials and access tokens are never embedded in this website, in public source code or in frontend code. Server-side keys are kept in secure server configuration.
  • We do not use your content to train models unless you have given explicit consent through the relevant application.
  • Where an AI provider's terms apply to the request, that provider's privacy policy also applies to the data processed by it.

9. Android and Flutter applications

ClueTech Technology applications may collect or process data that is separate from this website. Each application is described individually:

  • Application-specific privacy policy — every published application has its own privacy policy displayed on its Google Play listing, covering permissions, data types, storage and deletion.
  • Permissions — an application requests only the device permissions needed for its function (for example storage or photo access for a photo-editing tool).
  • On-device processing — wherever a task can be completed on your device, it is performed locally; data is sent elsewhere only when an online or AI service is required.
  • Applications in development — products shown as "Coming Soon" are not yet released and therefore do not yet collect user data.

If an application-specific policy exists, it prevails over this website policy for anything handled inside that application.

10. Data security

We take reasonable technical and organisational steps to protect information:

  • The website is served over HTTPS using encrypted transport.
  • Access to business email and devices is protected by passwords and, where available, multi-factor authentication.
  • No passwords, private API keys, database credentials or secret tokens are exposed in the website's frontend code.
  • Files are backed up according to our internal routine.

No method of transmission or storage is completely secure. If we become aware of a breach affecting your personal information, we will notify you and the relevant authorities as required by applicable law.

11. Data retention

  • Enquiry correspondence — kept only as long as needed to handle your request and maintain ordinary business records.
  • Construction project records — kept for the duration required for project documentation, warranty and statutory purposes.
  • Analytics data — retained in aggregate form for a limited period defined by the analytics provider.
  • Application data — retained as described in the relevant application's privacy policy; you can remove it by uninstalling the application or using its in-app delete option.

When data is no longer required, it is deleted or permanently anonymised.

12. Your rights

Subject to applicable law, you may have the right to:

  1. Request access to the personal information we hold about you.
  2. Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete information.
  3. Request deletion of information we no longer need to keep.
  4. Object to, or withdraw consent for, certain processing.
  5. Request information about how your data is processed.
  6. Lodge a complaint with a data protection authority.

Depending on your location, these rights may arise under laws such as India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 or equivalent legislation. To exercise any right, email contact@namoconstructions.in with the subject line "Data request". We will respond within a reasonable period and will verify your identity before acting on the request.

13. Children's privacy

This website is directed at business and consumer enquiries and is not intended for children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided information to us, please contact us and we will delete it.

14. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes in our website, applications or legal obligations. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page shows the most recent revision. Material changes will be highlighted on this page.
