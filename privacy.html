Privacy Policy

01 Scope of this policy

This Privacy Policy applies to this website — the pages, the contact form and any enquiry you make through it. It applies to Namo Constructions and to the technology division ClueTech Tecnnology, which is operated by the same organisation.

This policy does not apply to our mobile applications, which are distributed separately and may handle data differently. Those applications have their own application-specific privacy policies — see section 12.

02 Website enquiries

When you contact us through this website — by phone, by email, through the enquiry form or through a call-to-action link — we use the information you give us for one purpose: to respond to you and to discuss your requirement.

Typical enquiry information includes:

  • Your name
  • Your email address and phone number
  • The subject and content of your message
  • Details you choose to share about a plot, project or software requirement

This website is built with static HTML, CSS and JavaScript and does not include an advertising tracker, a marketing tag or a hidden analytics profile. If no form backend is connected, submitting the form simply prepares an email in your own email application; nothing is stored on the website itself.

03 Information we collect

Information you provide

Information you enter into the enquiry form or send to us directly. This is voluntary — we ask only for what is needed to respond to you.

Technical information collected automatically

Like most websites, our hosting provider may record standard server log information such as IP address, browser type, requested page, timestamp and referring page. This is used for security, troubleshooting and capacity planning.

What we do not collect

  • We do not ask for payment card details, bank account numbers or passwords.
  • We do not ask for identity documents through this website.
  • We do not build behavioural advertising profiles of website visitors.

04 Cookies and local storage

This website does not set advertising or tracking cookies. The site is designed to function without cookies, and it uses no cookie-based analytics by default.

If a form service provider is connected in future, that provider may use cookies or similar technologies for spam protection and to restrict abuse. Where that happens, this policy will be updated and any consent requirement will be added before the service is enabled.

Your browser or device may store standard technical data such as cache and preferences. You can clear or restrict this through your browser settings at any time without affecting your ability to read the site.

05 Analytics

We do not currently run website analytics tools. If privacy-friendly, cookie-free analytics are added later, they will be limited to aggregated page-view information, they will not build visitor profiles, and this section will be updated first.

If you submit an enquiry form, the form service provider that receives it may keep its own basic logs for abuse prevention, under that provider’s own privacy policy.

06 Advertising

This website carries no advertising, no sponsored placements and no affiliate links. We do not sell, rent or trade personal information, and we do not share enquiry details with advertisers.

If advertising is introduced in future, this policy will be amended to describe what is used and what choices you have before any advertising technology is activated.

07 Third-party services

When you use this website you may interact with the following external services:

  • Web hosting provider — stores and serves the website files and may keep standard server logs.
  • Email provider — handles messages sent to or from our business email address.
  • Google Maps — the “Directions” button opens Google Maps. If you follow it, Google applies its own privacy policy to that service.
  • Google Play Store — if you install one of our applications, the store applies its own privacy policy and data-safety disclosures to the installation and use of that application.
  • Form provider (optional) — if a form backend is connected, enquiries are transmitted to that provider, which acts as a processor on our instructions.

We do not control how third parties handle data once it reaches them.

08 Artificial intelligence APIs

Certain ClueTech Tecnnology applications use artificial intelligence to perform functions such as image resizing, subject detection or background removal. Those applications are distributed separately through Google Play and are governed by their own privacy policies.

Where an application uses a third-party AI or cloud service, note that:

  • Images or other inputs may be transmitted to the service provider to be processed.
  • Processing may take place on the provider’s servers, which can be located outside India.
  • Access keys or credentials for such services are never part of this website and are never visible in website source code. They are held only in secure server or application configuration.

Please read the in-app privacy information of any ClueTech application before you submit photographs or other personal content to it.

09 Data security

This website is served over HTTPS so that information you submit is encrypted in transit. Administrative access is restricted, and enquiry details are only accessed by the people who need them to respond to you.

No system is completely secure. You should not send sensitive personal information, financial details or identity documents through a website form. For anything sensitive, please contact us by phone.

10 Data retention

Enquiry details are kept only for as long as they are needed to respond to you and to maintain a record of our business correspondence. When they are no longer needed, they are deleted or anonymised.

Server log information is retained for the period required for security and troubleshooting, and is then cleared automatically by the hosting provider.

Records connected to a construction contract are retained for the period required by applicable law and for the purposes of after-sales service and dispute resolution.

11 Your rights

Subject to applicable law, you may ask us to:

  • Confirm what information we hold about you.
  • Provide a copy of that information.
  • Correct information that is inaccurate or incomplete.
  • Delete information we no longer have a reason to keep.
  • Withdraw consent where processing is based on consent.

To make any of these requests, contact us using the details in section 14. We may need to verify your identity before we act on a request. We will respond within a reasonable period.

12 Individual applications

Each Android application published by ClueTech Tecnnology has its own application-specific privacy policy and, where required by Google Play, its own Data Safety disclosure. Those documents take precedence over this website policy for information processed inside the application.

Application privacy practices may differ in important ways, for example:

  • An application may process images locally on your device, or may upload content to a cloud service to perform AI processing.
  • An application may collect usage and crash diagnostics to improve reliability.
  • An application may request permissions such as photo access, which you can withdraw in your device settings.
  • Data handled inside an application is not governed by this website’s retention periods.

Google Play requires every application to publish its own privacy policy URL. Please make sure each published application links to its own policy and completes the Data Safety section accurately before release.

13 Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time, for example when we change the website, add a service, or when legal requirements change. The “Last updated” date at the top of this page always reflects the current version.

Material changes will be indicated on this page. Continued use of the website after a change means the updated policy applies from that point onwards.
